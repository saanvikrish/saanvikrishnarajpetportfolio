Opening Legislative Panel

Mirror Your Representation ABE 2225 | 9:30 - 10:30 AM

The Latino Action Network welcomes New Jersey's Latino elected leaders and lawmakers. The conversation introduces these leaders to the community and explores their vision for New Jersey's Latino population, including how their leadership can advance equity and prosperity.

Moderator: Mel / Latino Student Council Annette Quijano Carmen Theresa Morales Alixon Collazos-Gill Gabriel Rodriguez Margo Chaly Frank Velez

Morning Sessions

Mental Wellness ABE 2200 | 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

This session explores how identity formation affects mental health and well-being among young Latinos by connecting sociocultural and psychological experiences. It examines the stress of negotiating ethnic identity in stigmatizing environments and identifies ways Latino communities support emotional well-being.

Moderator: Maria Montesino Dr. Gloria Lopez-Henriquez
Click here to see more

Immigration Session ABE 3200 | 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

A session focused on immigrant justice, lived experience, legal context, and community resources for families and advocates.

10 Steps to Advocacy ABE 4400 | 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

This session engages participants in practical steps any person or group can take to move from being concerned to becoming an effective advocate for change.

Participants will work in small groups to develop an advocacy plan for a fictional scenario involving a Latino-oriented arts organization whose state funding is being threatened.

David Matos, Jr. Tiana Pimentel

Afternoon Sessions

From Protest to Policy ABE 4400 | 12:30 - 1:30 PM

This workshop explores how protest and public advocacy can become meaningful legislative change. Participants will examine sustained organizing, coalition building, clear policy goals, outreach to elected officials, effective communication with decision-makers, and constituent mobilization.

Moderator: Cuqui Rivera Daniela Velez Erika J. Nava
